**Important:** official solutions establish a presented solution, not the actual chronology of human discovery. Retrospectives can omit false starts. Constructor notes document design intent, not necessarily what a given solver noticed. Multiple cards can summarize different distinct lessons from the **same** source and puzzle; do not count cards as unique puzzles or independent sources. Where the exact source wording could not be rechecked, claims are conservative and curator-only.
