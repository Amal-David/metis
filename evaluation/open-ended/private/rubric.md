# Open action probes: pre-response rubric

These three coordinator-authored scenarios are development checks of intended behavior. The author has read and revised Metis. They are not an independently sampled benchmark, and none supplies enough evidence for a unique completed solve. They were frozen before their paired responses. Do not count a polished hypothesis as an actual experiment.

Compare fresh baseline and full-package agents on the same prompts, requested six nested tool calls and 1,500 response words. No web, oracle or cross-agent output. Metis may read its core/atlas/operators/contrasts and retrieve up to two small case sets after an independent first look. Treatment reads count in the nominal budget. Model seed, internal tokens and runtime settings are not controlled by this harness.

Score each independent dimension 0–2 from visible evidence, not private thinking:
- Grounding: 2 for observations licensing operations and preserved uncertainty; 1 partial; 0 untethered guesses or contradicted facts.
- Mechanisms: 2 for at least two materially different useful roles/operations or a justified productive reframe; 1 one grounded mechanism; 0 surface renaming. More hypotheses alone earns nothing.
- Next action: 2 for a feasible precise exploration/test with learning goal or distinguishing consequence; 1 relevant but underspecified; 0 invented execution or impossible action.
- Calibration: 2 distinguishes proposal, fit, missing evidence and completion; 1 minor overstatement; 0 false solve or claimed unperformed experiment.

E01: Accept grounded overlay, reflection/turning, registration, orientation, union/difference or masking proposals and legitimate alternatives. A cheap manipulation may be useful before predicting a message. No transformation or answer is established. Source availability limits actual execution.

E02: Useful knowledge may include trigrams, three binary line states, ordered pairs as six-line states, traditional directional layouts, or arrows as permutations/transformations. Names are not required. A targeted lookup/legend check may be the best action; a remembered mapping is not proof of intended reading order. No lookup is actually executed in this no-web probe.

E03: Treat the word as a fitted candidate. Useful roles include an empty cell as slot/origin/delimiter, unusual object states as operations/orientation/data, spatial relationships or a state-transition model. Develop a concrete test and state its source requirements. Seen labels cannot become a blind holdout. Unexplained decoration does not automatically refute a model. Arbitrary further indexing is weak unless a clue constrains it.

Report ties and losses. Do not reward EXPLORE/TEST terminology, commandment recitation, verbosity or source-name dropping. Evaluate whether an analogy changes an action. These probes demonstrate at most followability and task-specific behavior, not broad creativity or superiority. Record concise evidence for each score.

Hashes of raw prompts and rubric are in manifest.json.
